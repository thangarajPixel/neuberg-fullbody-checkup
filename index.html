Frequently Booked Tests

At Neuberg Diagnostics, we offer a wide range of reliable and advanced blood tests designed to keep you informed and proactive about your health. Trusted by millions of patients, our most frequently booked tests include blood sugar (glucose test) to monitor diabetes, thyroid profile (T3, T4, TSH) to assess hormone balance, lipid profile to evaluate heart and cholesterol health, liver function test (LFT) to check liver performance, kidney function test (KFT) to measure kidney efficiency, complete blood count (CBC) to detect infections and overall immune health, and Vitamin D test to monitor bone strength and immunity.

Each test is performed with precision and advanced diagnostic technology, ensuring accurate, timely, and dependable results. Choose Neuberg Diagnostics for trusted blood tests near you—your partner in maintaining long-term wellness.
